Terms of Use

The short version: Sidewalk is a free community tool, not a government service. Posting a report doesn't contact the city for you. For emergencies, call 911. Be honest, be respectful, and follow the Community Guidelines.

These terms are an agreement between you and the Sidewalk team (Akshar Katta and Siddharth Tumati). By using Sidewalk, you agree to them. If you don't agree, please don't use Sidewalk.

What Sidewalk is, and isn't

  • Sidewalk is a student project that helps neighbors in North and South Carolina report and rally around local infrastructure problems.
  • It is not run by, or affiliated with, any city, county, state, or federal government agency.
  • Posting a report does not submit a service request to any government. Sidewalk can help you write a message to your city, but you choose whether and where to send it.
  • Endorsement counts and "council-ready" status are community signals. They are not official or legally binding petitions.

Not for emergencies

If anyone is in danger, call 911. For downed power lines, gas leaks, or water main breaks, call 911 or your utility right away. Don't wait for Sidewalk.

Who can use Sidewalk

  • You must be at least 13 years old to create an account. If you're under 18, you need a parent or guardian's permission.
  • One account per person. Use your real email address and keep your password private. You're responsible for what happens on your account.

Your reports and photos

  • You keep ownership of what you post.
  • By posting, you give Sidewalk permission to store, display, resize, and share your report publicly on Sidewalk, for as long as it's posted.
  • You confirm that you took the photos or have the right to share them, and that your report is honest to the best of your knowledge.
  • Reports are public. Don't include anything you wouldn't want the world to see.

Rules

You agree not to:

  • Post false, misleading, or joke reports.
  • Post spam, ads, or links, or promote a business, candidate, or campaign.
  • Harass, threaten, or insult anyone, or post hateful or sexual content.
  • Share someone's private information, or photos where people's faces or license plates can be identified.
  • Create extra accounts, use bots, or coordinate fake endorsements to inflate support.
  • Try to get around rate limits, the "I'm human" check, or suspensions, or try to break, overload, or scrape the service.
  • Break any law while using Sidewalk or taking photos for it.

Moderation

We may hide or remove reports, pause a report's progress for review, and suspend accounts or block networks that break these terms or the Community Guidelines. When several neighbors flag a report, it's hidden automatically until a coordinator reviews it. We try to be fair, but these decisions are ours to make.

AI features

Sideassist uses AI to answer questions and draft messages. AI can be wrong. Check everything before you rely on it or send it to anyone. See the AI Use Notice.

No guarantees

Sidewalk is provided "as is," for free. We can't promise that it will always be available or error-free, that information on it is accurate, or that any problem will get fixed. Reports are written by members of the public and aren't checked on site.

Limits on our responsibility

To the extent the law allows, the Sidewalk team isn't responsible for losses or damages from using Sidewalk, from content other people post, or from relying on AI-generated text. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have that can't be limited by law.

Ending your use

You can stop using Sidewalk and delete your account at any time from your account settings. We may suspend or end access for anyone who breaks these terms.

Changes

We may update these terms as Sidewalk grows. If we make important changes, we'll update the date at the top and note it in the app. Continuing to use Sidewalk means you accept the updated terms.

Questions?
