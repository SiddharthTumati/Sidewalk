Privacy Policy

The short version: we collect only what Sidewalk needs to work. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking cookies. Your name and email are never shown publicly. You can download or delete your data at any time from your account.

Sidewalk is a student project built by Akshar Katta and Siddharth Tumati for the Congressional App Challenge. It helps residents of North and South Carolina report problems like potholes and broken streetlights. This policy explains what information Sidewalk handles, why, and the choices you have. "We" and "us" means the Sidewalk team.

What we collect

When you create an account

  • Your display name and email address.
  • Your password, stored only as a scrambled one-way code (a "hash"). Nobody, including us, can read your actual password.
  • Whether you want email updates, and when your account was created and confirmed.

When you post a report

  • The title, description, and type of problem.
  • The spot you pin on the map and the area name (for example, "Columbia, SC").
  • Up to four photos. Photos are shrunk on your device, and hidden data inside them (like the GPS location and camera model) is removed before they're saved.
  • The report's progress timeline (for example, "Sent to the city").
  • If you're signed in, which account posted it. Guests can post without an account.

When you use Sidewalk

  • Which reports you endorse ("back"), follow, or flag.
  • A network fingerprint instead of your IP address. We don't store IP addresses in our database. When you post a report or endorse one, we store a scrambled one-way fingerprint of the network you're on. It lets us notice when many accounts come from one place to fake support, without being able to recover your actual address.
  • Your IP address is held briefly in memory to enforce rate limits (for example, how many reports can be posted per hour). These counters reset when the server restarts.
  • Our hosting provider keeps short-term technical logs of requests, which can include IP addresses. When someone uses the coordinator tools, the IP address is written to our security log.

When you use Cony (the AI helper)

  • What you type is sent to our AI provider to get an answer. Sidewalk does not save your chat messages or drafts. A conversation exists only in your open browser tab and disappears when you close it. See the AI Use Notice for details.

Your device and browser

  • One cookie: a sign-in cookie called `sidewalk_session` that keeps you logged in for up to 30 days. It's required for accounts to work. There are no analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies.
  • Your browser remembers which area of the map you chose, on your device only.
  • Your location is used only if you tap "Use my location," and only to drop the pin. We store the pin you post, not your location history.

How we use it

  • To run Sidewalk: show reports on the map, count endorsements, and track progress.
  • To send emails you need or asked for: confirming your email, resetting your password, and updates about issues you back or follow. You can turn updates off in your account or with the link in any update email.
  • To keep Sidewalk fair and safe: stopping spam, bots, and fake accounts, and reviewing flagged reports.

We never sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone for marketing.

What's public and what isn't

  • Public: each report's title, description, photos, map location, type, progress timeline, and how many neighbors back it.
  • Not public: your name, your email, which reports you posted, and who endorsed, followed, or flagged anything. Coordinators who moderate Sidewalk can see the name and email on a report's account if it's flagged.

Services that help run Sidewalk

These companies process some information for us, under their own privacy policies:

  • Railway hosts the app and its database.
  • Brevo delivers our emails. It receives your name, email address, and the message.
  • Groq runs the AI model behind Cony. It receives the text of your chats and drafts, and the text of new reports for the automatic clarity check. It does not receive your name, email, or photos.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile runs the "I'm human" check on sign-up, guest reports, and password resets. It looks at signals from your browser and device to tell people from bots.
  • OpenStreetMap provides the street map and place search. Esri provides the terrain map if you choose it. Google Fonts and unpkg deliver the fonts and map code. Your browser connects to these directly, so they can see your IP address and, for place search, what you searched for.

How long we keep it

  • Your account information stays until you delete your account.
  • Reports stay on the map until they're removed by the person who posted them, deleted with their account, or removed by a coordinator.
  • Sign-in sessions end after 30 days. Email confirmation links expire after 24 hours, and password reset links after 1 hour.

Your choices and rights

  • Download your data: open your account and choose Download my data to get a file with everything linked to your account.
  • Delete your account: open your account and choose Delete my account. This removes your account, endorsements, follows, and flags. You decide whether reports you posted are deleted too or stay on the map without any link to you.
  • Fix something: email us and we'll correct it.
  • Stop emails: turn off updates in your account or use the link in any update email.

We'll respond to any privacy request within 30 days.

Children

You must be at least 13 to create an account. If you're under 18, please have a parent or guardian read this policy with you. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Passwords are protected with a strong one-way hash, connections use HTTPS, and the app has rate limits, security headers, and moderation tools. No online service can promise perfect security, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll update the date at the top and note it in the app.

Questions?

Email siddharth.tumati@gmail.com. We read every message and reply within 30 days, usually much sooner.
