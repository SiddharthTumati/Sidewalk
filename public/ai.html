AI Use Notice

The short version: Sideassist uses AI to help you write and understand. It never decides anything on its own, it can be wrong, and Sidewalk doesn't save your chats.

Where Sidewalk uses AI

  • Ask Sidewalk (the chat button) answers questions about reporting local problems.
  • Sideassist turns your description of a problem into a clear, polite request you can send to your city.
  • Automatic clarity check: when a new report is posted, AI reads its text and adds an "AI reviewed" step to the timeline with a suggested urgency. This checks that the report is clear. It does not confirm that the problem exists.

Which AI we use

Sideassist currently uses an open AI model (OpenAI's GPT-OSS) hosted by Groq. The app can also be set up to use OpenAI or Google Gemini. If we change providers, we'll update this page.

What gets sent to the AI

  • Chat: your message and up to the last 12 messages of the conversation, so it can follow along.
  • Request drafter: the description and location you type.
  • Clarity check: a new report's type, area name, title, and description.

Never sent: your name, email address, password, photos, or exact map pin.

Sidewalk doesn't save your chat messages or drafts. They stay in your open browser tab until you close it. The AI provider processes them under its own terms and privacy policy. Please don't type private details, like your phone number or home address, into the chat.

What AI can't do

  • It can be wrong. AI sometimes states things confidently that aren't true. We tell it never to make up phone numbers, email addresses, laws, or agency names, but always check with your city's official website or 311 line.
  • It isn't legal advice.
  • It doesn't inspect anything. The "AI reviewed" step only looks at the words in a report.
  • It doesn't decide what matters. A report's priority comes from neighbors' endorsements and from coordinators, never from AI.
  • It doesn't act for you. Nothing is sent to your city automatically. You read every draft and choose whether to use it.

Fair use limits

To keep Sideassist free for everyone, there's a daily limit on AI requests per person and for the whole site. If you reach it, try again the next day.
